The flow

Five stages, each block with what it cost on this run - the corner of a block is its measured time, and the card says what that time was measured over. Dashed links hand a result from one stage to the next, the orange one is the only loop. Everything here runs unattended except the check, which a person makes by looking: when the mesh does not match the photographs, the answer is a better capture, another session or a longer one over what was missed. Click a block to jump to its card.

End to end: 99 min once per capture session, 66 min once per scene, then 3.0 h for every lens. This run - one session, 4 lenses - is 15.0 h of compute, of which 82 % is the lenses. A further 26 min runs in the background and holds nothing up.

What a reader should distrust

The metric scale is provisional: every metre on this page follows from reading the bay-separator pitch as 2.5 m, and a tape measure in the garage would fix it in an afternoon. The mesh carries one crater in the floor, about two metres across, that the walk crosses three times. And the clip models declare OPENCV_FISHEYE, whose reference implementation cannot evaluate the outer sixth of a 206 deg lens, so the depth there is correct but standard tooling will not reproduce it.
