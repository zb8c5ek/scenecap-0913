Three things to know before you trust a number. The metric scale is provisional: every metre follows from reading the painted bay pitch as 2.5 m, and a tape measure would settle it. The mesh carries one crater in the floor about two metres across, which the walk crosses three times. And the clip models declare OPENCV_FISHEYE, whose reference implementation cannot evaluate the outer sixth of a 206° lens, so the depth there is right but standard tooling will not reproduce it.
