What happened overnight

  • 19:57 the run started on the four sheet lenses: 3 clips × 4 passes × 5 phases each, pitches −2.5 / 0 / +2.5°, viewing axes at least 5° apart, two rounds.
  • seres_f3_right round 1 (to 23:04) was the result we wanted: clips at 4-34, 35-65 and 72-102 s, good frames 99.7 / 100 / 99.7 %, 20 renderings each with axes 5.1-6.0° apart, 17 314 views rendered with range maps.
  • The verifier ran without the carrier rule. run_direct.py built a fixed argument list for pd_verify, so it used the default --min_area_frac 0.0. Of its 27 confident hits, 25 cover 0.02-0.05 % of the frame - people at the far end of the aisle - and 2 are the operator reflected in a car window.
  • Round 2 fed all 27 back over 151 frames, dilated to 3 171 frame updates, and re-planned around them. Three clips became one, and the prune removed round 1's imagery. Round 1 now exists only as numbers in the summary and the journal.
  • byd_sq_back round 1 (to 03:21) repeated the good result - 3 clips, 100 / 100 / 99.7 % good, 17 434 views - then entered round 2, where the run ended at 03:23 with no error of any kind.
  • A second defect means the carrier flag alone would not have saved it: pd_verify measures a detection's box against the whole frame including the black ring outside the fisheye circle, so an edge artefact scores 5-9 % while a real distant person scores 0.03 %. On byd_sq_back 15 such artefacts sit above the 0.5 % bar. Until that is measured inside the valid mask, the setting to use is one round.
  • chery_ehy_left and jianghuai_x6s_front were never reached.
  • The follower session found the flag defect, fixed it, measured the damage and wrote it all down before it ended. Its fix is on its own branch, not yet on main.

Worth noting where the time actually goes: clip metrics and export together (119 min) cost more than the depth cast (90 min), and both are single-threaded loops over 17 400 independent views. The export also writes a private copy of the scene model into every clip folder - 311 MB of the 853 MB a finished rendering occupies, 19 GB per lens that could be one shared file.
