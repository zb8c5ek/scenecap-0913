Production is complete: four lenses, 240 clips, 69,459 frames each with a metric range map.

Every lens delivered three 30-second windows rendered twenty ways, all at 100 % good frames and rendered coverage 0.875 to 0.881. Frames and range maps pair one to one across the whole set, and no frame shows the person who carried the camera.

It took two attempts and one hardware fault to get here. The first run lost a lens to a missing flag, which let distant people collapse the second planning round; the fix was to pass the carrier rule to the verifier and run a single round. Both runs were then killed mid-stage by the external drive, which logged I/O retries and a delayed write failure while the director was streaming its 2.7 GB lattice by memory map. Moving that file to local storage ended it.

Along the way the two slowest post-processing stages were parallelised, which cut a lens from 4.4 h to 3.0 h and paid for itself within the first lens.

How it went

  • byd_sq_back was rescued rather than re-run: its renders survived the first run's death, so only the export was missing. 60 renderings recovered in 79 min without touching the GPU.
  • seres_f3_right, chery_ehy_left, jianghuai_x6s_front were run fresh with --rounds 1 and the carrier rule active. Verifier hits across the four lenses: 13, 600, 172 and 99, of which 2, 46, 6 and 3 reach the confidence bar - and the audit showed those are traffic cones, frame-edge slivers, parked cars and one reflection, not people.
  • The drive fault is documented and unresolved: G: reported disk retries and an exFAT delayed-write failure at 15:48, and the volume still reports Warning. The clips on it verified clean, but they are the only copy.
  • A quality audit of one finished lens measured the floor flat to 7.3 mm and level to 0.41°, two renderings of one instant agreeing to 2.5 mm, and poses bit-exact against the registration.

What is open

The material is delivered. These are the decisions it leaves.

1. Back up the clips — recommended

150 GB on one external drive that logged I/O errors today. There is no second copy.

a copy, and reseating the cable

2. Measure the metric scale

Every metre reported here follows from reading the bay pitch as 2.5 m. AprilTag boards at both ends of the next capture would replace the assumption with a measurement, and test the scale at both ends of the walk.

a printed board and ten seconds of filming

3. Spread the clips further

Within a window, twenty renderings share 96 to 98 % of what they see, because a 206° lens barely changes content when it rotates. Spending the same hours on more window starts and fewer angles each would make the set genuinely diverse.

a planner change, no new capture

4. Fill the mesh floor

12.3 % of the floor inside the scan region has no mesh surface, which is what the hole cost has been steering around. Filling it locally would remove the constraint and the crater the audit found.

a mesh pass, then a re-plan
